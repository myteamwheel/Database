## Important evidence limits

- TULIP's diagnostics are explicitly PARTIAL_LEDGER_ONLY, not a playable 240-minute rotation. Frozen DEV reduced form -0.127 pts/SD, AR 95% CI [-1.756, 1.021], does not validate direction or exact minutes as a win prescription. Chronological outcome holdouts are unspent.
- The minute-reconciliation holdout changed MAE by +0.0618 MPG, -0.0241 PTS, +0.0047 REB and +0.0096 AST. These are not evidence of a broad forecasting improvement and do not validate rookie/returner/injury forecasts.
- Roster membership is a dated snapshot, not confirmation of today's signing status. No unconfigured feed is represented as live.
- Ingestion may retain raw source caches after a failed owner run for diagnosis; public data/status/standalone artifacts are restored. This is not a transaction across all external sources or protection from process/power interruption.
